# Voucher Detail

Use this page to review a voucher after it has been created and confirm the amount, status, and linked party before reconciliation.

## Summary

The voucher detail page gives you the working record for a single voucher. Use it to verify what was entered, review notes, and confirm whether the voucher still allows edits.

## When to use this page

- When you need to review a voucher before posting or closing it.
- When you want to confirm the amount or reference value.
- When you need to check whether the voucher is still editable.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Trade -> Vouchers** and open any voucher from the list.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Vouchers Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Select the voucher you want to inspect.
1. Review the voucher details, notes, and status.
1. Update the voucher only if your role and the current status allow changes.

## Field reference

- **Voucher number** - The unique voucher reference.
- **Date** - The voucher date used for reporting.
- **Party** - The linked client, vendor, or other record.
- **Amount** - The financial value recorded on the voucher.
- **Status** - Shows whether the voucher is still open or already finalized.
- **Notes** - Internal explanation or supporting details.

## Tips and common issues

- Verify the voucher status before editing, because later states may lock changes.
- If the amount or party looks wrong, check the original entry before making adjustments.
- Keep notes concise and specific so another user can understand the transaction later.
