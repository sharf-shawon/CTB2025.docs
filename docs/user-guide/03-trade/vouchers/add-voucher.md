# Add Voucher

Use this page to create a voucher record for a financial entry that is not handled through an invoice or payment workflow.

## Summary

Create vouchers when you need a controlled record for money in or money out that should still appear in finance tracking. Accurate voucher entry helps your team reconcile balances and follow internal approval rules.

## When to use this page

- When you need to record a non-invoice financial transaction.
- When you want a paper trail for finance adjustments.
- When you need to track an amount against a vendor, client, or internal account.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Trade -> Vouchers** and click the add action on the list page.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Vouchers Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Click the add action on the list page.
1. Enter the voucher details and amount.
1. Add notes if the reason for the voucher needs to be explained.
1. Click **Save** to store the record.

## Field reference

- **Voucher number** - The unique reference for the voucher.
- **Date** - The date the voucher was created.
- **Party** - The client, vendor, or other record connected to the voucher.
- **Amount** - The value recorded by the voucher.
- **Status** - Indicates whether the voucher is draft, posted, or closed.
- **Notes** - Internal explanation for why the voucher was created.

## Tips and common issues

- Use a clear voucher number so the finance team can trace the record later.
- Add notes whenever the voucher is linked to an unusual correction or adjustment.
- Review the amount carefully before saving, because it affects downstream balances.
