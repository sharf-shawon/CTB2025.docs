# Add Bank

Use this page to register a bank account in CTB Admin so your team can use it for checks, vouchers, payments, and reconciliation.

## Summary

Create a bank record when your business starts using a new account or needs to track an existing account inside the system. A complete bank record helps users choose the right account when recording transactions.

## When to use this page

- When you open a new business bank account.
- When you need to add an account used for checks or vouchers.
- When you want to prepare an account for payment reconciliation.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Trade -> Banks**. On the Banks list page, click the add button.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Banks Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Click the add action on the list page.
1. Enter the bank details you want CTB Admin to store.
1. Review the account information before saving.
1. Click **Save** to create the record.

## Field reference

- **Bank name** - The name of the bank that holds the account.
- **Account number** - The exact account number used for transactions.
- **Branch** - The branch that manages the account.
- **Opening balance** - The starting balance if you want CTB Admin to track it from day one.
- **Notes** - Optional internal details about how the account should be used.
- **Status** - Indicates whether the account is active for new transactions.

## Tips and common issues

- Enter the account number carefully, because users will rely on this record when selecting a bank later.
- Mark the account inactive if it is closed or should no longer be used.
- Keep notes short and operational so staff can quickly understand the account's purpose.
