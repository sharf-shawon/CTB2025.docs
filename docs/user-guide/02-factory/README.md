# Factory Module

Use the Factory module to manage product categories, raw materials, stock movement, and finished products.

## Summary

The Factory section supports production and inventory work. Use it to keep material stock accurate, define product structure, and review the records that feed business and trade workflows.

## What you can do in this section

- Manage product categories.
- Track raw materials and material movement.
- Record inventory in and out entries.
- Maintain product definitions and costing.

## Module structure

| Module             | Purpose                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Categories         | Group materials and products for easier management. |
| Materials          | Track raw material stock and movement history.      |
| Material Inventory | Review stock entries and changes.                   |
| Products           | Define finished products, pricing, and costing.     |

## Typical workflow

1. Create the category first.
1. Add materials and track their inventory movement.
1. Define products and link the required materials.
1. Review stock and costing before releasing product records for use.

## Related sections

- Business - Supplies client and vendor records used by other modules.
- Trade - Uses products in invoices, checks, and payments.
