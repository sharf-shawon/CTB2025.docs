# Business Module

# Business Overview

The **Business** section in CTB Admin manages all external parties your organization interacts with. It is divided into two main modules: **Clients** and **Vendors**.

This section acts as the central place for storing, updating, and analyzing all customer and supplier information used across transactions, payments, and reports.

______________________________________________________________________

## What you can do in this section

- **Manage clients** who place orders and receive invoices
- **Manage vendors** who supply goods or services
- **Store contact and business information** for all parties
- **Track financial relationships** including balances and limits
- **Access transaction history** linked to clients and vendors
- **Control active/inactive status** for operational use

______________________________________________________________________

## Module structure

| Module  | Purpose                                                          |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clients | Manage all buyers, their invoices, payments, and balances        |
| Vendors | Manage all suppliers, purchases, payments, and financial records |

______________________________________________________________________

## How it fits into the system

The Business section connects directly with financial and trade operations:

- **Trade → Invoices** — Linked with Clients
- **Trade → Purchases / Vouchers** — Linked with Vendors
- **Trade → Payments** — Applies to both Clients and Vendors
- **Reports** — Generate financial summaries based on client/vendor data

______________________________________________________________________

## Typical workflow

1. **Add a Client or Vendor** before starting any transaction
1. **Use them in transactions** (Invoices for clients, Purchases for vendors)
1. **Record payments** to update balances
1. **Review details and reports** for financial tracking

______________________________________________________________________

## Key difference between Clients and Vendors

| Aspect          | Clients           | Vendors              |
| --------------- | ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Role            | Buyers            | Suppliers            |
| Money Flow      | Receive money     | Pay money            |
| Used In         | Invoices          | Purchases / Vouchers |
| Balance Meaning | Amount receivable | Amount payable       |
