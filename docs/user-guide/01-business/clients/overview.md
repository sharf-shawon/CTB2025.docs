# Clients Overview

The **Clients** module in CTB Admin manages all buyer and business-partner records used across the system. A client is any individual or organization that places orders or receives invoices. Each client record stores identifying information, contact details, financial limits, and discount settings that are applied automatically when invoices, payments, and reports are generated.

## What you can do in this module

- **Register clients** with contact details, NID documentation, and business information.
- **Manage active status** — enable or disable clients to control whether they appear in new invoice dropdowns.
- **Configure financial limits** — set upper and lower balance limits and maximum discount rates per client.
- **Control SMS notifications** — choose which clients receive automated invoice SMS messages.
- **View client history** — access all past invoices, payments, and balance changes from the Client Detail page.
- **Analyze client data** — use the Client Reports page for financial summaries and trends.

## Typical workflow

1. **Add** a new client before creating their first invoice.
1. **Edit** the client record when contact details, limits, or status change.
1. **View the Client Detail** page to check balances and invoice history at any time.
1. **Run Client Reports** for periodic financial reviews.

## Related modules

- **Trade → Invoices** — Invoices are linked to client records and inherit their discount and SMS settings.
- **Trade → Payments** — Payments are applied to client balances.
