# Salary Detail

Use this page to review one salary record in detail and verify the final amount before it is paid.

## Summary

The salary detail page shows the breakdown behind a single salary record. Use it to confirm earnings, deductions, attendance inputs, and payout status before you close the payroll cycle.

## When to use this page

- When you need to confirm a salary calculation.
- When you want to review earnings and deductions for one employee.
- When you need to check whether a salary has already been paid.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Employee -> Salary** and open a salary record from the list.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Salary Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Select the salary record you want to inspect.
1. Review the employee details and salary breakdown.
1. Confirm the payout status before making any changes.

## Field reference

- **Employee** - The staff member tied to the salary record.
- **Month** - The payroll month used for this salary.
- **Earnings** - Base pay and any additional earnings included in the salary.
- **Deductions** - Amounts subtracted from the gross pay.
- **Net salary** - The final amount owed to the employee.
- **Status** - Shows whether the salary is pending, generated, or paid.

## Tips and common issues

- Review the breakdown carefully before you mark a salary as paid.
- If the amount is incorrect, check the attendance or wage source rather than editing the final record first.
- Keep salary detail records unchanged after payment unless your payroll process allows corrections.
