# Generate Salary

Use this page to generate monthly salary records for one or more employees.

## Summary

Generate salary after attendance, wage, and deduction data are ready. This workflow creates the salary records your finance or HR team will review before payout.

## When to use this page

- At the end of a payroll cycle.
- After attendance and wage records have been checked.
- When you need to create salary records for a department or group of employees.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Employee -> Salary** and open the salary generation page.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Salary Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Start a new salary generation run.
1. Select the payroll month or period.
1. Choose the employees or department to include.
1. Review the salary calculation before saving.
1. Save the generated records.

## Field reference

- **Payroll month** - The salary period being created.
- **Employee or department** - The staff group included in the run.
- **Attendance source** - The records used to calculate salary, where applicable.
- **Earnings** - Base salary or wage amounts included in the total.
- **Deductions** - Reductions applied before the final pay amount.
- **Net amount** - The salary amount to be paid.

## Tips and common issues

- Confirm attendance data before generating salary so you do not have to recalculate later.
- Generate one payroll period at a time to keep records easy to audit.
- If salary amounts look off, inspect the wage or attendance source first.
