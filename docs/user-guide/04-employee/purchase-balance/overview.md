# Purchase Balance Overview

Use this page to review employee purchase balances and understand how much each employee still owes or has available in the ledger.

## Summary

Purchase balance tracking helps the business monitor employee purchases, deductions, and repayments in one place. Use this page when you need a ledger-style view of employee balances.

## When to use this page

- When you need to check an employee's current balance.
- When you want to review purchase-related deductions or repayments.
- When you need to confirm whether a balance is still open.

## How to access this page

From the sidebar, go to **Employee -> Purchase Balance**.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Purchase Balance Overview** from the sidebar.
1. Review the balance list for the employee you need.
1. Open the record if you need more transaction detail.
1. Compare the balance with the related salary or payout records when needed.

## Field reference

- **Employee** - The staff member whose balance is being tracked.
- **Opening balance** - The amount carried into the current period.
- **Purchases** - Employee purchases recorded against the ledger.
- **Repayments** - Amounts already recovered or settled.
- **Current balance** - The amount still outstanding.

## Tips and common issues

- Use clear notes so repayment adjustments are easy to audit later.
